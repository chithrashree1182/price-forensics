## MRP credibility — 2026-08-15, single-day sample

An MRP is declared by the manufacturer and printed on the pack, so it is a
property of the product. A genuine catalogue therefore shows roughly one
distinct MRP per product.

| | Snapdeal | Reliance Digital |
|---|---|---|
| Products sampled | 38 | 22 |
| Distinct MRP values | **9** | **21** |
| Diversity ratio | **0.24** | **0.95** |
| Top-5 concentration | **89%** | 27% |
| Median advertised discount | **79%** | 9% |
| Verdict | inconsistent with product-specific pricing | consistent |

Most-repeated claimed MRPs on Snapdeal:

- Rs 999 — claimed by 14 different products
- Rs 1,499 — claimed by 13 different products
- Rs 1,999 — claimed by 4 different products

**The larger advertised saving sits on the less product-specific set of
reference prices.**

### Limitations

- **Small sample, single day.** 60 products total. Directional, not definitive.
- **Different populations.** Snapdeal's sampled categories are unbranded
  accessories; Reliance's are branded electronics. Some of the gap may reflect
  category and seller mix rather than pricing conduct alone.
- **No claim about any individual MRP.** The argument is about a distribution.
  It cannot show a specific price is false, only that a set of them does not
  behave the way product-specific prices behave.
- The round-number rate is reported for context and deliberately excluded from
  the verdict — genuine retailers price at Rs 23,999 routinely.
